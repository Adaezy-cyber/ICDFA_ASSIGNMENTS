## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable and prohibited use of NexusTech information systems, devices, networks, applications, accounts, and information resources. It is intended to reduce security, privacy, operational, and compliance risks while allowing employees and authorised third parties to perform their roles effectively.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all NexusTech employees, contractors, consultants, interns, and other authorised users who access NexusTech systems, devices, networks, applications, or information.

It applies to company-owned and authorised personally owned devices when they are used to access NexusTech resources.

## 3. Acceptable Use

Users must:

1. Use NexusTech systems and information only for authorised business purposes and legitimate work-related activities.
2. Protect their authentication credentials and never share passwords, MFA approvals, tokens, or other authentication information.
3. Use only accounts and systems for which they have been authorised.
4. Handle company and client information according to its classification and applicable security requirements.
5. Report suspected security incidents, phishing, unauthorised access, data loss, or accidental disclosure promptly through the approved reporting channel.
6. Lock or secure devices when unattended.
7. Use approved storage, collaboration, communication, and file-sharing services for company and client information.
8. Follow applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements when working remotely or travelling.

## 4. Prohibited Use

Users must not:

1. Attempt to gain unauthorised access to systems, accounts, applications, networks, or information.
2. Share accounts or authentication credentials with another person.
3. Disable, bypass, or interfere with security controls without explicit authorisation.
4. Install unauthorised software or tools that create unacceptable security or licensing risk.
5. Use NexusTech resources to conduct unlawful activities, harassment, fraud, or activities that could materially damage the organisation or its clients.
6. Store or transfer sensitive company or client information using unapproved personal cloud storage, personal email, or other unauthorised services.
7. Copy, disclose, modify, or remove company or client information without appropriate authorisation.
8. Use company resources to intentionally introduce malware or other malicious content.

## 5. Reasonable Personal Use

Limited personal use may be permitted where it:

- is lawful and does not interfere with work responsibilities;
- does not create material security, privacy, legal, or reputational risk;
- does not involve prohibited activities;
- does not consume excessive organisational resources; and
- does not involve unauthorised storage or sharing of company/client information.

NexusTech may restrict personal use where business or security requirements require it.

## 6. Remote and Mobile Working

Users working remotely or travelling must take reasonable measures to protect company and client information, including:

- avoiding unsecured public networks where practical;
- using approved secure access methods;
- preventing unauthorised persons from viewing sensitive information;
- keeping devices physically secure; and
- reporting lost or stolen devices promptly.

## 7. Monitoring and Privacy

NexusTech may monitor, log, or review use of its systems and information resources where permitted by applicable law and organisational requirements. Monitoring may be used to support security, investigations, service management, compliance, and protection of company and client information.

Users should have no expectation that use of organisational systems is completely private, subject to applicable law and company requirements.

## 8. Roles and Responsibilities

### Users

Users are responsible for following this policy, protecting credentials and information, using systems appropriately, and reporting suspected incidents.

### IT and Information Security

IT and Information Security are responsible for implementing relevant technical and administrative controls, providing security guidance, monitoring where authorised, and supporting incident handling.

### Managers

Managers are responsible for ensuring that users have access appropriate to their roles and supporting policy awareness and compliance within their teams.

### Human Resources

HR supports employee communication, onboarding/offboarding requirements, and appropriate personnel actions where policy violations occur.

### Compliance / Legal

Relevant compliance or legal stakeholders provide advice on regulatory, contractual, privacy, and legal requirements affecting acceptable use.

## 9. Compliance and Enforcement

Failure to comply with this policy may result in access restrictions, investigation, remedial training, disciplinary action, contract consequences, or other appropriate action, subject to applicable law and organisational procedures.

Suspected serious or repeated violations may be escalated to Information Security, management, HR, Legal, or Compliance as appropriate.

## 10. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented, justified by a legitimate business need, risk-assessed, approved by the appropriate authority, and time-limited where practical.

Users must not create informal exceptions by bypassing controls.

## 11. Approval and Review

This policy is approved by the designated executive authority and owned by the Information Security Manager.

The policy is reviewed at least annually and earlier when significant changes occur, including material technology changes, regulatory or contractual changes, significant incidents, or identified control weaknesses.
