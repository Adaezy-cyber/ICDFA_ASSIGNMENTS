## Executive Summary

TechGlobal is a 2,500-employee technology organization operating across five global offices. Its security governance has not matured at the same pace as the business. Security decisions are concentrated around the IT Director, business units make inconsistent local decisions, formal governance committees do not exist, and executive or Board visibility into cyber risk is limited.

This portfolio redesigns that model around clear decision rights, independent risk challenge, cross-functional governance, documented escalation, and auditable accountability.
As The Lead Security Governance Consultant, this project transitions TechGlobal from an informal, IT-centric security model  to a scalable, cross-functional, and accountable governance framework designed for a 2,500-employee organization operating across five global offices.

## Design Principles

1. One accountable owner per decision or activity wherever practicable.
2. Risk acceptance belongs with the authorised risk owner, not automatically with the technical implementer.
3. The CISO provides security leadership and challenge without becoming the sole owner of enterprise risk.
4. Business units remain accountable for business outcomes while operating inside enterprise security requirements.
5. Committees make decisions within defined authority; they do not replace named accountable executives.
6. Material cyber risk has a defined escalation route to executive management and the Board.
7. Important decisions leave an evidence trail: issue, risk, options, decision, authority, action owner, due date, and closure evidence.

## Summary of Governance Artifacts Included
**1. Governance Architecture (Task 1)**
- Identified 5 critical weaknesses in TechGlobal's current informal model (Concentration of authority, absence of committees, blind spots for the Board, siloed business units, undefined escalations).

- Built a comprehensive Stakeholder Map covering the Board, CEO, CISO, CRO, Legal, Finance, HR, IT, and Business Units.

- Designed a hierarchical Organisation Chart ensuring proper reporting lines and segregation between business operations and risk oversight.

**2. Responsibilities & Decision Rights (Task 2)**
- Established role profiles for the 8 core governance roles detailing their purpose, core responsibilities, decision authority, reporting lines, and KPIs.

- Formulated conflict-resolution principles for overlapping authorities (e.g., CISO vs. IT Director, CISO vs. Business Unit Leads).

**3. Committee Ecosystem (Task 3)**
- Established the Executive Security Council (Strategic oversight), Security Governance Steering Committee (Operational cross-functional governance), and Specialized Working Groups (Cloud & TPRM).

- Provided complete Terms of Reference (ToR), a sample meeting agenda, a working decision log, and a 12-month recurring governance calendar.

**4. RACI Accountability Matrix (Task 4)**
- Mapped 15 mandatory governance activities across all key stakeholders ensuring a strict "single Accountable role" rule per activity.

- Wrote an implementation guide for managers governing planning, approvals, and incident management phases.

**5. Risk Escalation & Segregation of Duties (Task 5)**
- Designed a 3-level Cyber-Risk Escalation Workflow (Operational, Executive, and Material/Board) with clear thresholds and response times.

- Populated a Segregation-of-Duties (SoD) Weakness Register identifying 5 high-risk conflicts in the legacy model and providing practical corrective controls.
