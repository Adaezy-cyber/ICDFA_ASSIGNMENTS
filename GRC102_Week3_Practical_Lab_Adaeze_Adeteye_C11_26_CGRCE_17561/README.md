# TechGlobal Governance Transformation Simulation

**GRC102 – Information Security Governance | Module 3: Roles and Responsibilities in Security Governance**

## Executive Summary

TechGlobal is a 2,500-employee technology organisation operating across five global offices. Its security governance has not matured at the same pace as the business. Security decisions are concentrated around the IT Director, business units make inconsistent local decisions, formal governance committees do not exist, and executive/Board visibility into cyber risk is limited.

This portfolio redesigns that model around clear decision rights, independent risk challenge, cross-functional governance, documented escalation, and auditable accountability.

## Design Principles

1. One accountable owner per decision/activity wherever practicable.
2. Risk acceptance belongs with the authorised risk owner, not automatically with the technical implementer.
3. The CISO provides security leadership and challenge without becoming the sole owner of enterprise risk.
4. Business units remain accountable for business outcomes while operating inside enterprise security requirements.
5. Committees make decisions within defined authority; they do not replace named accountable executives.
6. Material cyber risk has a defined escalation route to executive management and the Board.
7. Important decisions leave an evidence trail: issue, risk, options, decision, authority, action owner, due date, and closure evidence.
