## 2. Role Profiles

### Board
**Purpose:** Oversight, not operational security management.  
**Responsibilities:** Challenge material risk, review major incidents and assurance, monitor management response.  
**Authority:** Board-level risk appetite and material matters.  
**Reporting:** Receives concise decision-oriented cyber-risk reporting.  
**KPIs:** Scheduled report timeliness; Board-requested action closure.

### CEO
**Purpose:** Align cyber governance with business strategy.  
**Responsibilities:** Sponsor security governance, resolve executive trade-offs, ensure resources, escalate material risk.  
**Authority:** Executive risk and investment decisions within delegation.  
**KPIs:** Strategic security delivery; material-risk decision timeliness.

### CISO
**Purpose:** Lead security governance and security programme direction.  
**Responsibilities:** Strategy, policies, security risk analysis, metrics, architecture oversight, incident governance.  
**Authority:** Security requirements and programme decisions within delegation.  
**KPIs:** Critical-risk treatment timeliness; control-effectiveness trend.

### CRO / Risk
**Purpose:** Provide independent enterprise-risk challenge.  
**Responsibilities:** Risk methodology, aggregation, challenge and enterprise reporting.  
**Authority:** Risk governance within the enterprise framework.  
**KPIs:** Risk-assessment currency; independent challenge rate.

### Legal / Compliance
**Purpose:** Account for legal, regulatory, contractual and privacy implications.  
**Responsibilities:** Regulatory analysis, notification advice, contracts and evidence requirements.  
**Authority:** Legal/regulatory advice and required sign-off.  
**KPIs:** Regulatory assessment timeliness; obligation coverage.

### Finance
**Purpose:** Govern security investment and financial exposure.  
**Responsibilities:** Investment cases, cost challenge, exposure analysis and prioritisation.  
**Authority:** Budget decisions within delegation.  
**KPIs:** Budget execution; major investment business-case coverage.

### HR
**Purpose:** Integrate people governance into security.  
**Responsibilities:** JML, awareness, conduct and disciplinary support.  
**Authority:** Personnel-process decisions within HR authority.  
**KPIs:** JML timeliness; awareness completion.

### IT
**Purpose:** Implement and operate approved technology controls.  
**Responsibilities:** Architecture implementation, remediation, access, recovery and evidence.  
**Authority:** Technical implementation within approved requirements.  
**KPIs:** Critical vulnerability remediation; successful change implementation.

## 3. Conflict Resolution

**IT vs Risk Acceptance:** IT supplies technical evidence; CISO assesses security; CRO challenges the decision ; the authorised risk owner accepts or rejects residual risk.

**CISO vs CRO:** CISO owns security analysis; CRO owns enterprise-risk methodology and challenge. Material disagreement is documented and escalated rather than resolved informally.

**Business Growth vs Security:** Board explains business need; CISO assesses security; Legal or Compliance advises; CRO assesses enterprise exposure; authorised executive decides.

**Finance vs Security Investment:** Finance challenges cost or value assumptions; CISO documents risk or control need; CEO resolves strategic trade-offs; material residual risk remains visible.
